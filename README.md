## 🛫 **Flight Booking System - Entity Relationship Diagram Project**

An innovative and structured **Flight Booking System** designed for efficient airline operations. This project models the key processes of airline management — including flight scheduling, passenger reservations, seat management, service add-ons, and payment processing — using a well-defined and advanced entity-relationship (ER) diagram.

---

### 📁 **Project Files**

- **flight-booking-entity-diagram.png** – A detailed ER diagram illustrating the workflow and structure of the flight booking system.
- **flight-management-overview.pptx** – A presentation providing a step-by-step walkthrough of the system's entities and relationships.

---

### 🧩 **Diagram Highlights**

The diagram illustrates the primary components and interactions of the flight booking system, including:

- **System Roles**: Passengers, operators, and airline administrators.
- **Flight Management**: Scheduling flights, tracking routes, and managing seat assignments.
- **Reservation Workflow**: Booking seats, managing passenger data, and maintaining reservation statuses.
- **Payment Integration**: Handling transactions for reservations and tracking payment statuses.
- **Add-On Services**: Managing additional services like luggage, meals, and in-flight entertainment.
- **Database Relationships**: Well-defined links between entities (e.g., passengers, flights, payments) for data integrity.

The diagram serves as a blueprint for developing secure, scalable, and efficient airline reservation systems.

---

### 🎯 **Project Objectives**

This project was created as part of a design and modeling exercise to produce a realistic and functional airline reservation system. The objectives include:

1. Streamlining the flight booking lifecycle for passengers and staff.
2. Providing a robust structure for managing flights, seats, payments, and services.
3. Offering a scalable model for implementing airline software with advanced features.
